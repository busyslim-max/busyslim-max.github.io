Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Popsy ("we", "the app") is a social media scheduling and publishing tool. This policy explains what data Popsy handles and how.

What we collect

  • Your Popsy account details: email address and name, used to sign in.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect an account (for example Pinterest), the platform gives Popsy an access token and, where offered, a refresh token, plus basic profile information such as your username, account ID and the list of your boards or pages.
  • Content you create in Popsy: post text, titles, links, images and videos you upload, schedule times, and the publishing results (such as the ID of the created post or an error message).

How we use it

  • Only to publish the content you choose, to the accounts and boards you choose, at the time you choose, and to show you the status of your posts.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • Content is sent only to the social media platform you publish to, through its official API.
  • Uploaded media is made available to a platform through a private, signed, time-limited link only when that platform needs to fetch it.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored on servers controlled by the Popsy developer. Access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Connections use HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

  • You can disconnect a social account in Popsy at any time.
  • You can also revoke Popsy's access in the platform's own settings (for Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins / Apps).
  • To delete your Popsy account and all its data, email busyslim@gmail.com and it will be removed within 30 days.
